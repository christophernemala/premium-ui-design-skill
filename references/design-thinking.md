# Design Thinking for Clean, Serene, Pixel-Perfect 3D SaaS Websites

## Core Aesthetic Goal
Create websites that feel **clean, serene, pixel-perfect, and luxurious**.  
Quiet confidence. Space to breathe. Cinematic depth without visual noise.  
High-resolution imagery and motionographics that support the product story rather than compete with it.

## How to Think
1. **Start with silence** — Begin with near-black or soft off-white canvas and generous whitespace. Add only what earns its place.
2. **One strong idea per section** — Hero with one cinematic 3D/shader moment. Product sections with clear hierarchy. Never stack competing effects.
3. **Craft over generation** — Prefer 21st.dev real design-engineer components (shaders, glass, liquid metal, heroes) over inventing new ones.
4. **Physics feels real** — Springs settle naturally. Camera moves are slow and intentional. Objects have mass.
5. **Pixel-level care** — Align to 4/8px grid. Hairline borders. Precise tracking on display type. High-res assets only.
6. **Serene motion** — Motion should feel like breathing or water, never like a carnival. Low bounce, measured duration, purposeful stagger.
7. **SaaS product clarity** — Every visual decision must ultimately serve understanding the product value. Cinematic elements frame the product; they do not replace clear messaging, pricing, or CTAs.

## Framework Presentation Order (How to Show the Stack)
When building or documenting a project, present the system in this order:
1. **Tokens first** — Colour system, type scale, spacing, radius, shadows (or absence of them)
2. **Layout system** — Grid, max-widths, section rhythm, mobile breakpoints
3. **Component library** — 21st.dev + shadcn primitives, themed
4. **Motion system** — Spring presets + reduced-motion strategy
5. **3D / Shader layer** — R3F scenes, post-processing pipeline, performance budget
6. **Content & imagery** — 4K assets, microcopy, real photography or product renders

## Typical SaaS Page Structure (Serene Cinematic)
- Hero: large quiet canvas + one refined 3D/shader moment + clear value prop + primary CTA
- Social proof / logos (subtle)
- Product showcase: interactive 3D or high-res stills with soft motion
- Feature sections: bento or clean cards, generous spacing
- Pricing / CTA
- Footer: minimal

## Keys to Non-AI Output
- Real authors and real code from 21st.dev
- Tight typography tracking and optical alignment
- Controlled metallic / glass / liquid accents instead of random gradients
- Consistent surface ladder and hairlines
- Intentional camera and spring physics instead of default easings
- High-resolution, carefully cropped imagery
